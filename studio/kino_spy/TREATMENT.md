# OPERATSIYA: STARS — 60s Spy Title Sequence, 1080x1920, 24 fps, ~29 s, Uzbek

## Three structures
1. **Chase** — an agent chases a golden star through locations built from credits; the star becomes the logo. (Classic; close to the genre cliché.)
2. **Countdown** — "3 bosish": three locations, each a numbered click, a bomb-clock built from type. (Strong but thin on services.)
3. **Briefing → mission → title** (CHOSEN) — a tape briefing hands the agent a dossier; each line of the briefing *becomes a set* (object, price, method, coverage), the agent moves through them on the beat, and the object (a star) is finally placed into the StarsFuel badge cut-out: the object of the chase is the title. Chosen because the product facts (100 Stars = 22 000 so'm, 3 taps, Premium/Gift/PUBG UC) ARE the briefing, so every credit carries a real fact.

Opening image: a single golden star on a black backing, locked on a mustard ground (cold open, mystery). Ending: diagonal grid pieces fly back and assemble STARSFUEL; the star drops into the badge; final stab freeze on @StarsFuelBot.

## Logline / arc
An agent receives a mission — get 100 Telegram Stars cheap and fast — and pulls it off in three taps. Setup (briefing, quiet tape) → turn (the drop: price slams in, the chase starts) → ending (the star is set into the StarsFuel badge; title).

## Benchmark
Saul Bass (*North by Northwest*, *Anatomy of a Murder*): flat inks, type as architecture, diagonals. Kuntzel+Deygas (*Catch Me If You Can*): cut-paper lateral chase. Learn: hard cuts on stabs, credits that stop dead on beats, silhouettes small against type. Do not take: any real title's lettering, characters, shots, the famous spy theme.

## Inks
ink #15170f · bone #ece6d6 · hot green #6fa83a (agent's scarf, danger/accent; StarsFuel green) · mustard #e2a52a (the star = object). Grounds per location: mustard (cold open), bone (briefing), green (price), ink-dark #2a2d22 (3 taps), mustard (coverage train), bone→ink (title).
